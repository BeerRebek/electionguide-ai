# Braille & Accessibility in Voting

## Braille Features on EVMs
- Braille markings on the blue voting buttons of the balloting unit
- Braille labels for candidate serial numbers
- Available on all M3 model EVMs since 2014
- Enables visually impaired voters to identify candidate positions

## Polling Station Accessibility
- Ramps mandatory at all polling stations
- Ground-floor locations preferred for accessibility
- Wheelchairs available at polling stations
- Magnifying glasses available for voters with low vision
- Separate queues for elderly (80+) and disabled voters

## Companion Voting (Rule 49N)
- Voters who are blind or physically infirm may take a companion into the voting compartment
- The companion must be over 18, an elector, and not a polling agent
- The companion takes an oath of secrecy
- The Presiding Officer records companion details in the register

## Postal Ballot for Disabled Voters
- Voters with physical disability preventing travel to polling station can apply for postal ballot
- Application through Form 12D
- Voters above 80 years also eligible for postal ballot
