# Candidate Expense Limits

## Current Limits (2024)
- **Lok Sabha**: ₹75 lakh to ₹95 lakh (varies by state and constituency size)
- **State Assembly**: ₹28 lakh to ₹40 lakh (varies by state)
- Limits revised periodically by ECI based on cost inflation

## What Counts as Election Expenditure
- Public meetings and rally costs
- Vehicle hire and fuel
- Campaign material (posters, pamphlets, banners)
- Advertisement costs (print, TV, radio, digital)
- Travel expenses
- Loudspeaker and sound system rentals
- Food and refreshments at campaign events

## Monitoring Mechanisms
- **Expenditure Observer**: Senior IRS/IAS officer appointed by ECI
- **Flying Squads**: Check vehicles for cash, liquor, and freebies
- **Video Surveillance Teams**: Record campaign events for expense estimation
- **Shadow Register**: ECI maintains its own estimate of each candidate's spending
- **Daily expense register**: Candidates must maintain and submit for inspection

## Penalties for Overspending
- **Section 10A, RP Act**: Disqualification for 3 years
- **Section 77**: Failure to file expense account → disqualification for 3 years
- Criminal prosecution for corrupt practices under Section 171H IPC
