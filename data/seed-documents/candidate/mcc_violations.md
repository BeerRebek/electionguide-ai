# Model Code of Conduct Violations

## Common Violation Categories

### Category 1: Hate Speech & Communal Appeals
- Appeals on basis of religion, caste, race, community, or language
- Incitement to violence or public disorder
- **Penalty**: FIR under Sections 153A/295A IPC; campaign ban of 24-72 hours

### Category 2: Misuse of Government Machinery
- Using official vehicles, buildings, staff for campaign purposes
- Government advertisements during MCC period
- Inaugurating new projects or making policy announcements
- **Penalty**: Show-cause notice to party/candidate; possible adverse action

### Category 3: Inducement (Cash/Liquor/Gifts)
- Distributing cash, liquor, or gifts to voters
- Organizing free transportation to polling stations
- **Penalty**: FIR under Section 171B IPC (bribery); seizure of materials

### Category 4: Defacement
- Putting up posters, banners without permission
- Using walls, fences, trees for campaign material
- **Penalty**: Fine and removal at candidate's cost

## Reporting Mechanism: cVIGIL App
- Citizens can report violations via photo/video with GPS coordinates
- Report received by District Control Room within **5 minutes**
- Flying Squad dispatched for verification within **15 minutes**
- Action initiated within **100 minutes** of complaint
- Status updates sent to complainant on the app

## ECI's Powers
- Issue warnings and censure to candidates/parties
- Order removal of offensive content
- Bar candidates from campaigning for specified periods
- Direct filing of FIR by police
- Refer cases to courts for contempt proceedings
