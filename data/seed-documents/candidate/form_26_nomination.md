# Form 26 — Candidate Affidavit

## Mandatory Disclosures

### Criminal Cases
1. All cases pending before any court (FIR number, sections of law, court details)
2. All cases where charges have been framed by a court
3. Cases of conviction
4. Cases where the candidate has been acquitted
5. Whether the offences are cognizable or non-cognizable

### Financial Disclosure
1. **Assets**: Movable and immovable assets of:
   - Self
   - Spouse
   - Dependents
2. **Liabilities**: Outstanding loans from banks, financial institutions, and individuals
3. **Sources of income**: Profession, salary, business income
4. **PAN details**: PAN card number

### Educational Qualifications
- Highest educational qualification (from 5th standard to PhD)
- Self-declaration (no certificate required)

### Other Details
- Age, address, party affiliation
- Whether holding any government contract

## Nomination Process

### Step-by-Step
1. Obtain nomination form from the Returning Officer's office
2. Fill in Form 2A (nomination paper) with personal details and proposer details
3. Prepare Form 26 affidavit (sworn before a Magistrate or Notary Public)
4. Deposit security amount:
   - **Lok Sabha**: Rs. 25,000 (Rs. 12,500 for SC/ST candidates)
   - **Assembly**: Rs. 10,000 (Rs. 5,000 for SC/ST candidates)
5. File nomination during the designated filing period (between 11 AM and 3 PM)
6. Maximum **4 nomination papers** can be filed per candidate
7. At least one nomination must have a **proposer** who is a registered elector of the constituency

### Scrutiny
- The Returning Officer examines nominations on the date fixed for scrutiny
- Grounds for rejection:
  - Proposer not a registered elector of the constituency
  - Candidate is not qualified (age, citizenship)
  - Candidate is disqualified under any law
  - Incomplete Form 26 or nomination paper
  - Security deposit not paid

### Withdrawal
- Candidates may withdraw nomination by a signed notice before the deadline
- Final list of contesting candidates published after withdrawal date

## Expense Monitoring

### Expense Limits (2022 Revision)
- **Lok Sabha**: Rs. 95 lakh per candidate
- **Assembly elections**: Rs. 40 lakh per candidate (varies by state)
- **North Eastern states and UTs**: Lower limits apply

### What's Included in Expenses
- Travel expenses (vehicle hire, fuel)
- Printing and distribution of pamphlets, posters
- Public meeting costs (venue, sound system, stage)
- Media advertisements
- Campaign workers' payments
- Any other election-related expenditure

### Monitoring Mechanism
- Candidates must maintain a **day-to-day account** in a prescribed format
- Submit final account to the DEO within **30 days** of result declaration
- Election Expenditure Observers (nominated by ECI) monitor spending
- Video surveillance teams document campaign activities
- Shadow observation registers maintained by the district administration
