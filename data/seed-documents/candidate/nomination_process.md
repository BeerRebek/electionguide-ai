# Nomination Process for Candidates

## Eligibility to Contest
- Indian citizen
- Age: 25+ years for Lok Sabha/Assembly; 30+ years for Rajya Sabha/Legislative Council
- Registered as voter in any constituency in India
- Not disqualified under RP Act (bankruptcy, government contract, corrupt practices conviction)

## Steps to File Nomination
1. Obtain nomination form from Returning Officer's office
2. Get 10 registered electors from the constituency as proposers
3. Pay security deposit: ₹25,000 (Lok Sabha) / ₹10,000 (Assembly); half for SC/ST candidates
4. Complete Form 26 affidavit (criminal, financial, educational disclosure)
5. Submit to Returning Officer during nomination period
6. Up to 4 nomination forms can be filed per candidate

## Form 26 Affidavit Requirements
- Pending criminal cases with details (case number, court, charges, stage)
- Cases where convicted (sentence, whether appeal pending)
- Assets: movable and immovable property (self, spouse, dependents)
- Liabilities: loans, dues, guarantees
- Educational qualifications
- PAN card details
- Income Tax returns for last 5 years

## Scrutiny & Withdrawal
- Scrutiny on the day after last nomination date
- RO verifies eligibility, proposer validity, deposit, Form 26 completeness
- Candidates can raise objections about other candidates
- Withdrawal period: 2 days after scrutiny
- Final candidate list published after withdrawal deadline
- Symbols allotted by Returning Officer

## Security Deposit Forfeiture
- Deposit forfeited if candidate gets less than 1/6th of total valid votes
- Deposit refunded within 30 days if candidate polls more than 1/6th
