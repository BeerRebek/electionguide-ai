# Form 17C — Account of Votes

## Part I (Filled at Polling Station)
- Total electors assigned to the polling station
- Total voters who actually voted (from Form 17A)
- Number of tender votes
- Number of challenged votes
- Signed by Presiding Officer and all polling agents

## Part II (Filled During Counting)
- Candidate-wise vote tally as displayed on EVM
- Total valid votes
- NOTA votes
- Signed by counting supervisor and candidates' counting agents

## Importance
- **Primary audit document** linking polling station to final result
- Candidate agents receive certified copies after Part I completion
- Discrepancy between Part I total and Part II total triggers investigation
- Available as evidence in election petition proceedings
- Must be preserved by Returning Officer for minimum 1 year
