# Form 20 — Declaration of Result

## Contents
- Constituency name and number
- Total valid votes polled
- Candidate-wise vote count (including NOTA)
- Number of rejected postal ballots
- Name of winning candidate
- Winning margin
- Signed by Returning Officer with date and time

## Declaration Process
1. All counting tables submit their tallies
2. Returning Officer verifies tabulation against Form 17C records
3. Postal ballot results added to EVM results
4. Final totals computed
5. Result announced verbally at counting center
6. Form 20 prepared, signed, and copies distributed

# Forms 21C and 21E — Certificate of Election

## Form 21C (State Assembly)
- Issued by Returning Officer to winning candidate
- Contains: Candidate name, party, constituency, votes secured
- Enables the elected member to take oath of office

## Form 21E (Lok Sabha)
- Similar to Form 21C but for Parliamentary constituencies
- Forwarded to Secretary General, Lok Sabha
- Required before taking oath as Member of Parliament

## After Declaration
- Results transmitted to ECI headquarters via ENCORE software
- Official gazette notification published
- Election petitions can be filed within 45 days of result declaration
