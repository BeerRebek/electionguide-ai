# Counting Procedures & Result Declaration

## Pre-Counting

### Strong Room Opening
1. Candidates/agents invited to witness opening of the strong room
2. All seals checked and verified to be intact
3. CCTV footage of strong room reviewed if any concerns raised
4. EVMs transported to counting hall tables

### Counting Center Setup
- Multiple counting tables (typically 14 per Assembly segment)
- Each table has a Counting Supervisor appointed by the Returning Officer
- Each candidate can appoint one counting agent per table
- Observers from the Election Commission present

## Counting Process

### Round-by-Round Counting
1. EVMs arranged in order of polling station serial numbers
2. For each EVM, the Returning Officer or designated official:
   a. Verifies the seals on the Control Unit carrying case
   b. Opens the case and switches on the Control Unit
   c. Presses the **RESULT** button
   d. The display shows the number of votes polled for each candidate
   e. The result is recorded on a result sheet
3. Results from each table are compiled after every round (typically 14 EVMs per round)
4. Round-wise results displayed on a public display board

### VVPAT Verification (5 Random Stations)
1. Before electronic counting begins, **5 polling stations** are randomly selected per Assembly segment
2. Selection done by **lottery** in the presence of candidates/agents
3. VVPAT drop boxes from these stations are opened
4. Paper slips physically counted by hand
5. Count compared with the EVM electronic result for that station
6. If **match**: Counting proceeds normally
7. If **discrepancy**: The VVPAT paper count is taken as the correct result for that station

### Form 17C Reconciliation
- The total votes shown on each EVM must match the total recorded in Form 17C Part I (prepared by the Presiding Officer after polling)
- Any mismatch is investigated before proceeding

## Result Declaration

### Form 20 — Final Result Sheet
- After all rounds are counted, the Returning Officer prepares Form 20
- Form 20 records:
  - Total votes polled
  - Votes received by each candidate
  - Rejected votes (if postal ballots involved)
  - The candidate declared elected

### Form 21C — Declaration of Result
- The Returning Officer publicly declares the result
- Issues certificate of election (Form 22) to the winning candidate

### Form 21E — Return of Election
- Comprehensive statement of votes polled submitted to the Election Commission

## Postal Ballots
- Postal ballots are counted **first**, before EVM counting begins
- Postal ballot categories: Service voters, persons on election duty, persons in preventive detention, persons with disability, voters above 80 years
- The postal ballot counting result is added to the EVM tally
- A postal ballot is rejected if: not received in the proper envelope, signature doesn't match, received after the deadline, or the voter has also voted at the polling station

## Recounting
- Any candidate can demand a recount if the margin of victory is narrow
- The Returning Officer may order a recount if they are not satisfied with the accuracy of any particular round
- Recounting involves pressing the RESULT button again on the EVMs and re-verifying
- There is no limit on the number of recounts that can be requested
