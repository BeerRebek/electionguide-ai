# Form 6 — Application for Inclusion in Electoral Roll (New Voter Registration)

## Who Should Apply
- Indian citizens who have turned **18 years of age** on or before the qualifying date
- The qualifying date is **January 1st** of the year of revision of the electoral roll (changed from October 1st by amendment in 2023)
- Citizens who have shifted residence to a new constituency
- Citizens whose names are not on any electoral roll

## How to Apply

### Online Application
1. Visit the **National Voter Service Portal**: https://voters.eci.gov.in
2. Click on "New Voter Registration" / "Form 6"
3. Fill in personal details: Name, father/mother/husband name, date of birth, gender
4. Upload required documents
5. Enter the address of ordinary residence (where you currently live)
6. Select your constituency (auto-detected from address)
7. Upload a recent passport-size photograph
8. Submit the application
9. Note down the **Reference ID** for tracking

### Offline Application
1. Obtain Form 6 from the office of the Electoral Registration Officer (ERO) or download from ECI website
2. Fill in the form in English or the regional language
3. Attach self-attested copies of required documents
4. Submit to the ERO or the Booth Level Officer (BLO) of your area
5. Alternatively, submit at **Voter Facilitation Centers** set up at every Assembly constituency

### Through Voter Helpline App
1. Download the **Voter Helpline App** from Google Play Store or Apple App Store
2. Register using your mobile number
3. Navigate to "Form 6" section
4. Fill in details and upload documents
5. Submit through the app

## Required Documents

### Proof of Age (Any One)
- Birth certificate
- Class 10 (SSC/SSLC) marksheet or certificate
- Indian passport
- PAN card
- Aadhaar card
- Driving license

### Proof of Address (Any One)
- Aadhaar card
- Indian passport
- Driving license
- Utility bills (electricity, water, gas — not older than 3 months)
- Bank/Post Office passbook
- Latest Income Tax Assessment Order
- Ration card
- Latest rent agreement with receipts

### Photograph
- Recent passport-size photograph (JPEG, max 200KB for online)
- White background preferred

## Verification Process
1. After submission, the ERO assigns the application to the **Booth Level Officer (BLO)**
2. The BLO conducts a **field verification** visit to the applicant's address
3. The BLO verifies the applicant's identity, age, and residence
4. The BLO submits a verification report to the ERO
5. If verified successfully, the ERO includes the name in the electoral roll
6. The applicant receives the **EPIC (Electors Photo Identity Card)** — commonly known as Voter ID card

## Important Timelines
- **Continuous registration**: Applications accepted throughout the year
- **Special revision**: During election season, a special revision period allows bulk registrations
- **Processing time**: Typically 15-30 days after BLO verification
- **EPIC delivery**: Within 30-45 days of approval

## Tracking Application Status
- **Online**: Visit https://voters.eci.gov.in → Track Application Status
- **SMS**: Send EPIC <space> Reference ID to 1950
- **Helpline**: Call **1950** (toll-free) for status updates

## After Registration
- Your name appears in the **Part I** of the electoral roll for your polling station
- You receive your EPIC with your photo, name, father/husband name, address, and unique EPIC number
- You can verify your name in the roll by searching on the NVSP portal
- Your polling station is assigned based on your residential address
