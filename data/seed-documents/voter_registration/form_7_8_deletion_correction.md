# Form 7 — Objection for Deletion from Electoral Roll

## Purpose
To raise an objection against the inclusion of a name in the electoral roll, requesting its deletion.

## Common Reasons for Filing Form 7
1. The person has **died**
2. The person has **shifted** from the constituency permanently
3. **Duplicate entries** in the same or different electoral rolls
4. The person is **not an Indian citizen**
5. The person is **not of sound mind**

## Who Can File
- Any registered elector of the same constituency
- Family members of the deceased person

## Required Information
- Name and EPIC number of the person whose deletion is sought
- Part number and serial number in the electoral roll
- Reason for seeking deletion
- Supporting evidence (death certificate, proof of shifting, etc.)

## Process
1. File Form 7 online at NVSP or submit to ERO
2. ERO issues notice to the person concerned (if alive)
3. Hearing conducted if objections are raised
4. ERO decides on deletion after inquiry

# Form 8 — Correction of Entries in Electoral Roll

## Purpose
To correct any errors in an existing entry in the electoral roll.

## Types of Corrections (Form 8)
- **Name correction**: Spelling errors in name, father's/husband's name
- **Age correction**: Wrong date of birth
- **Gender correction**: Incorrect gender recorded
- **Address correction**: Wrong address details
- **Photo update**: Replace old or incorrect photo
- **EPIC replacement**: Apply for a new EPIC card

## Form 8A — Transposition (Shifting within Same Constituency)
If a voter has moved to a new address **within the same constituency**, Form 8A is used to transfer the registration to the new polling station area without going through fresh registration.

## Required Documents
- Self-attested copies of supporting documents for the correction
- Current EPIC card copy
- Proof of new address (for Form 8A transposition)

## Process
1. Submit Form 8 or 8A online at NVSP or to ERO
2. BLO may conduct field verification for address changes
3. Corrections updated in the electoral roll
4. New EPIC issued if required
