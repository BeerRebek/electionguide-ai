# Form 8 — Correction of Entries

## What Can Be Corrected
- Name spelling errors
- Father's/Mother's/Husband's name
- Date of birth / Age
- Gender
- Address (within the same constituency)
- Photo replacement
- EPIC number errors

## How to Apply
- **Online**: voters.eci.gov.in or Voter Helpline App
- **Offline**: Submit to BLO or Electoral Registration Officer
- **Voter Service Centers**: Facilitation centers in every constituency

## Required Documents
- Copy of existing EPIC (Voter ID)
- Supporting document for the correction (e.g., Aadhaar for name, birth certificate for DOB)
- Recent passport-size photo (for photo update)
- Self-declaration form

## Processing Timeline
- Application verified by BLO through field visit
- ERO approves/rejects within 15-30 days
- Corrections reflected in next supplement of electoral roll
- Updated EPIC issued within 30 days of approval
