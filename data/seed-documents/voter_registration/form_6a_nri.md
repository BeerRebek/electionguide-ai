# Form 6A — NRI Voter Registration

## Eligibility
- Must be an **Indian citizen** (not a citizen of any other country)
- Must hold a **valid Indian passport**
- Must be residing abroad (outside India)
- Must have attained the age of **18 years** as on the qualifying date (January 1st)
- Name should not be in any electoral roll in India

## Application Process
1. Visit the **National Voter Service Portal**: https://voters.eci.gov.in
2. Select "Overseas Voter Registration" → Form 6A
3. Fill in personal details and passport information
4. Enter the address as mentioned in the passport (this determines the constituency)
5. Upload scanned copies of passport (front and back pages)
6. Upload a recent passport-size photograph
7. Submit and note the Reference ID

## Voting Rights
- NRI voters are registered in the electoral roll of the constituency where their **passport address** falls
- Currently, NRI voters must be **physically present** at the assigned polling station in India to vote
- The ECI has proposed extending postal ballot facilities to NRI voters (pending legislative approval)
- NRI voters are marked with "O" (Overseas) in the electoral roll

## Important Notes
- The registration is valid as long as the passport is valid
- If the NRI returns to India permanently, they should apply for transfer to their new address using Form 8A
- NRIs can appoint a proxy voter through a specific application process (if legislation is passed)
