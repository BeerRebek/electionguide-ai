# Document Requirements for Voter Registration and Voting

## Documents for Voter Registration (Form 6)

### Proof of Age (Any One)
1. Birth certificate issued by Municipal authority or Registrar of Births and Deaths
2. Marksheet or certificate of Class 10 (SSC/SSLC/Matriculation)
3. Indian Passport
4. PAN card
5. Aadhaar card/letter
6. Driving License
7. Certificate from Medical Board stating age (in the absence of above)

### Proof of Address (Any One)
1. Aadhaar card/letter
2. Indian Passport
3. Driving License
4. Bank/Post Office current passbook
5. Ration card
6. Water/telephone/electricity/gas connection bill (not older than 3 months)
7. Latest Income Tax Assessment Order
8. Rent agreement with receipts
9. Latest property tax receipt
10. Post/letters received through India Post at the address

### Photo Requirements
- Passport-size photograph (2" × 2")
- Recent photograph with white background
- For online upload: JPEG format, max 200KB

## Documents for Polling Day Identification (Any One of 12)

The ECI has approved the following **12 photo identity documents** for voter identification at polling stations:

1. **EPIC** (Electors Photo Identity Card / Voter ID)
2. **Aadhaar Card** (UIDAI)
3. **Indian Passport**
4. **Driving License**
5. **PAN Card**
6. **Service Identity Card** (issued by Central/State Government, PSU, Public Limited Companies)
7. **Bank/Post Office Passbook** with photograph
8. **MGNREGA Job Card**
9. **Health Insurance Smart Card** (issued under RSBY scheme)
10. **Pension document with photograph**
11. **Official identity card issued to MPs, MLAs, MLCs**
12. **Smart Card issued by RGI** (Registrar General of India) under National Population Register (NPR)

## Important Notes
- Voters can present **ANY ONE** of the 12 documents — it is not mandatory to carry the EPIC
- The voter's name must be present in the **electoral roll** of the polling station
- If the voter's name is in the roll but they have no photo ID, the Presiding Officer may allow them to vote under specific provisions
- **Indelible ink** is applied to the left forefinger after voting — a voter with ink already applied will be denied voting
