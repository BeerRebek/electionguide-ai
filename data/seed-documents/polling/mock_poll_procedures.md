# Mock Poll Procedures

## Mandatory Requirements (Rule 49MA)
- Conducted at **every polling station** on poll morning
- Minimum **50 votes** must be cast (at least 1 per candidate + NOTA)
- All polling agents invited to observe and participate

## Process
1. Presiding Officer announces beginning of mock poll
2. Votes cast by polling agents and officers across all candidate buttons
3. Record of votes cast maintained on paper
4. After 50 votes, Close button pressed
5. EVM result display compared with paper record
6. VVPAT slips extracted, counted, and compared
7. If CU display = paper record = VVPAT count → **Mock poll successful**
8. **Mock Poll Certificate** printed and signed by all agents
9. **Clear button** pressed — resets all counts to ZERO
10. Zero reading displayed and confirmed by all agents
11. Machine sealed for actual polling

## If Mock Poll Fails
- Machine immediately replaced with reserve unit
- Fresh mock poll conducted on replacement machine
- Defective machine sealed and sent for examination
- Incident reported to Returning Officer and ECI Observer
- Detailed entry made in Presiding Officer's diary
