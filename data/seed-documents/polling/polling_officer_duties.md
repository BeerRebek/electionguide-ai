# Polling Officer Duties

## Polling Team Composition
- **Presiding Officer**: Overall in-charge of the polling station
- **Polling Officer 1**: Voter identification and entry in Form 17A
- **Polling Officer 2**: Indelible ink application and checking
- **Polling Officer 3**: Control Unit operation (Ballot button)

## Pre-Poll Duties
- Arrive at polling station by 5:30 AM
- Set up EVM, VVPAT, and voting compartment
- Verify all seals with polling agents
- Conduct mandatory mock poll (minimum 50 votes)
- Display zero count to all agents

## During Polling
- Verify voter identity (EPIC or approved ID)
- Check left index finger for existing ink mark
- Record voter details in Form 17A
- Apply indelible ink to left index fingernail
- Issue ballot slip
- Press Ballot button on Control Unit to enable voting
- Handle special cases: tender votes, challenged votes, assisted voters

## Post-Poll Duties
- Announce close of poll
- Press Close button; note total votes on control display
- Seal EVM in carrying case with green paper seal
- Package all forms, unused ballot papers, voter slips
- Transport sealed EVM to collection center under escort
