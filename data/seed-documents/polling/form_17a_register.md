# Form 17A — Register of Voters

## Purpose
- Official record of every person who votes at a polling station
- Primary audit trail linking voter identity to vote count
- Used for turnout calculation and EVM cross-verification

## Contents
- Voter serial number (from electoral roll)
- Name and father's/husband's name
- Gender
- EPIC (Voter ID) number or alternative ID details
- Voter's signature or thumb impression
- Polling Officer's initials
- Remarks column (for tender votes, challenged votes, etc.)

## Procedures
- Polling Officer 1 maintains Form 17A throughout poll day
- Each voter must sign or provide thumb impression before being allowed to vote
- Serial numbers are called out — polling agents can note them
- Entries are sequential and cannot be altered after recording
- Total entries in Form 17A must match the total votes on the EVM

## Post-Poll Use
- Returned to Returning Officer in sealed envelope
- Form 17A count compared with EVM total at counting
- Available for inspection during election petition proceedings
- Retained as official record for minimum 1 year
