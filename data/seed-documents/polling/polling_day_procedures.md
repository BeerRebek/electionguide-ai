# Polling Day Procedures

## Pre-Poll (Before 7 AM)

### Setting Up
1. Presiding Officer and polling team arrive at least **90 minutes** before poll starts
2. Set up the polling station as per layout prescribed by ECI
3. Ensure proper lighting, signage, ramps for disabled voters, and drinking water
4. Set up EVM (Control Unit and Balloting Unit) and VVPAT
5. Display candidate names and symbols on the notice board outside

### Mandatory Mock Poll
1. Conducted in the **presence of all polling agents** of candidates
2. At least **50 test votes** must be cast on the EVM
3. Agents and Presiding Officer together decide which candidate gets each test vote
4. After all test votes, the RESULT button is pressed
5. EVM results are verified against the recorded test votes
6. VVPAT slips are also counted and verified
7. Only if mock poll is **100% accurate**, EVM is cleared for actual voting
8. EVM is then **CLEARED** (all test votes erased) and sealed
9. Mock poll results are recorded in the Presiding Officer's diary

## During Poll (7 AM — 6 PM)

### Voter Flow
1. Voter enters polling station, joins queue
2. **Polling Officer 1**: Checks voter's identity against electoral roll using voter's EPIC or any of 12 approved IDs
3. **Polling Officer 2**: Applies **indelible ink** to the left forefinger nail and checks for existing ink
4. **Polling Officer 3**: Gets voter's signature/thumb impression in **Form 17A** (Register of Voters)
5. Presiding Officer presses **BALLOT** button on Control Unit
6. Voter enters voting compartment, presses blue button for chosen candidate
7. VVPAT displays paper slip for **7 seconds** for verification
8. Voter exits polling station

### Special Situations
- **Challenged votes**: If identity is challenged by a polling agent, voter takes oath and pays Rs. 2 deposit
- **Tendered votes**: If someone has already voted in the voter's name, the real voter casts a "tendered vote" on a paper ballot (not counted in EVM)
- **Persons with disabilities**: Companion allowed in voting compartment to assist
- **Transgender voters**: Can vote in any queue of their choice

### Indelible Ink
- Applied to the **left forefinger** nail
- Contains silver nitrate — stays for 48-72 hours
- Prevents duplicate voting
- Must be checked before allowing entry to the polling station

## Post-Poll (After 6 PM)

### Sealing Procedures
1. Presiding Officer announces poll closed
2. Allows voters already in queue to complete voting
3. Presses **CLOSE** button on Control Unit — no more votes can be recorded
4. Prepares **Form 17C Part I** — Account of votes (total votes polled, etc.)
5. Gets signatures of polling agents on Form 17C
6. Seals the Control Unit result section with **pink paper seal**
7. Places Control Unit in carrying case and seals with self-locking seals
8. Seals the VVPAT drop box
9. Records all seal numbers in diary
10. Allows polling agents to affix their seals on the carrying case
11. Packs unused EVM materials, register of voters, and other papers

### Transportation to Collection Center
- Sealed EVMs transported under **armed police escort**
- Presiding Officer accompanies the materials
- Materials handed over to the Returning Officer at the collection center
- EVMs stored in **strong rooms** under 3-tier security
