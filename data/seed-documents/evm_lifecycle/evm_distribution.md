# EVM Distribution to Districts and Constituencies

## Three-Tier Randomization Process

### Tier 1: State to District
- **Software-based randomization** using ECI's proprietary algorithm
- Conducted in the presence of ECI General Observer
- Political party representatives invited as witnesses
- **Randomization certificate** generated and signed by all parties
- Machines are assigned district codes but not specific locations

### Tier 2: District to Assembly Constituency
- Conducted at the district headquarters
- **District Election Officer (DEO)** supervises the process
- Second round of software randomization
- Representatives of all contesting candidates invited
- Results displayed on screen and printed for all present

### Tier 3: Constituency to Polling Station
- Final randomization at the Returning Officer's level
- Conducted 5-7 days before poll day
- **ECI Observer** must be present for this round
- Serial numbers of assigned EVMs recorded against each polling station
- Information shared with all contesting candidates

## Transportation Protocols

### Vehicle Requirements
- **Government vehicles only** — no private transport for EVMs
- Each vehicle accompanied by a **government escort officer**
- **GPS tracking** enabled on all transport vehicles
- Sealed containers with tamper-evident seals
- **Route map** pre-approved by DEO

### Chain of Custody
1. Warehouse in-charge signs dispatch note
2. Transport officer acknowledges receipt with serial numbers
3. Receiving officer at destination verifies serial numbers
4. Any discrepancy reported immediately to DEO and ECI Observer
5. Complete trail documented in EVM Tracking Software

## Reserve EVMs
- **20% reserve** maintained at each distribution center
- Reserve machines undergo same randomization process
- Deployed only if primary machine fails during mock poll or on poll day
- Replacement requires written authorization from Returning Officer
