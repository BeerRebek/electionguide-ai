# EVM Commissioning (Candidate Setting)

## When Commissioning Happens

- **After final candidate list** is published (post-withdrawal of nominations)
- **Before second randomization** of EVMs to polling stations
- Typically **7-10 days before poll day**
- Must be completed before mock poll preparations begin

## The Commissioning Process

### Step 1: Ballot Paper Setting
- Returning Officer prepares the **ballot paper order** based on:
  - Recognized national party candidates (listed first)
  - Recognized state party candidates
  - Registered unrecognized party candidates
  - Independent candidates
  - Order within each category is alphabetical
- **NOTA** (None of the Above) option placed at the end

### Step 2: Loading Candidate Data
1. **Candidate slips** (with name, party, symbol) prepared and printed
2. Slips inserted into the **ballot paper screen** on each Balloting Unit
3. Each candidate button aligned with the corresponding candidate slip
4. Up to **16 candidates per Balloting Unit** (maximum 4 BUs = 64 candidates)

### Step 3: VVPAT Configuration
1. Candidate data loaded into VVPAT printer module
2. Test print of each candidate slip verified for:
   - Correct name
   - Correct party symbol
   - Correct serial number
   - Print clarity and alignment
3. Paper roll installed and sealed

### Step 4: Verification
- **Each contesting candidate or agent** can verify:
  - Their name and symbol appear correctly
  - Button press produces correct VVPAT slip
  - No cross-mapping of buttons to wrong candidates
- Any errors corrected on the spot
- **Commissioning certificate** signed by all present

## Sealing After Commissioning

### Inner Seals
- **Address tag seal**: Unique paper seal with polling station details
- **Thread/strip seal**: Around the CU-BU cable connector
- **Special tag**: Signed by candidates' agents

### Seal Register
- Every seal number recorded in the **Seal Register**
- Cross-referenced with EVM serial number and polling station
- Candidates receive a copy of seal numbers for their polling stations

## Storage After Commissioning
- Commissioned EVMs stored in **strong room** at distribution center
- Strong room under 24/7 CCTV and double-lock system
- No access without written authorization from Returning Officer
- EVMs dispatched to polling stations 1-2 days before poll day
