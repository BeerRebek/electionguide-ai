# EVM Storage During Non-Election Periods

## Storage Facility Requirements

### Physical Infrastructure
- **Dedicated warehouse** at district headquarters or designated location
- Minimum area: 500 sq ft per 500 EVM units
- **Anti-rodent measures**: Sealed entry points, mesh on ventilation
- **Fire safety**: Fire extinguishers, smoke detectors, fireproof storage racks
- **Climate control**: Temperature maintained between 15°C and 35°C; humidity below 70%

### Security Requirements
- **24/7 CCTV surveillance** with minimum 30-day recording retention
- **Double-lock system**: DEO holds one key, Treasury Officer holds another
- **Armed guard** posted outside the warehouse at all times
- **Access log** maintained — every entry/exit recorded with purpose, time, and authority
- **Biometric access** at main entry (implemented in some states)

## Maintenance Schedule

### Quarterly Checks
1. Visual inspection of all units for physical damage
2. Battery status verification
3. Seal integrity check
4. Cleaning and dusting
5. Random functional testing (5% sample)

### Annual Maintenance
1. Complete functional testing of every unit
2. Battery replacement if below threshold
3. Firmware verification (hash check against master record)
4. Environmental testing (temperature, humidity response)
5. Documentation update in EVM tracking software

## Inventory Management

### EVM Tracking Software (ETS)
- Every EVM has a unique **serial number** tracked in centralized database
- **Location tracking**: Warehouse → District → Assembly → Polling Station
- **Status tracking**: Serviceable / Under Repair / Condemned
- **History**: Complete lifecycle log from manufacturing to disposal
- Real-time dashboard accessible to ECI headquarters

### Condemnation Process
- EVMs older than 15 years (M3) or showing defects are condemned
- Condemnation committee: 3 members including technical expert
- Condemned units destroyed under videography at authorized facility
- Destruction certificate issued and recorded
