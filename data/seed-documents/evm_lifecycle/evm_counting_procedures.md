# EVM Counting Procedures

## Counting Day Setup

- Government building designated as counting center with 14 tables per hall
- Three-tier security cordon; mobile phones prohibited inside
- Returning Officer, AROs, counting supervisors, candidates' agents, ECI Observer, and micro-observers present

## Process

### Step 1: Seal Verification
- Strong room opened under videography with candidates present
- Counting supervisor verifies seals against Form 17C Part I
- Any discrepancy → machine set aside for special scrutiny

### Step 2: Result Display
- Result button pressed on Control Unit; votes per candidate shown
- Form 17C Part II filled with candidate-wise count; signed by supervisor and agents

### Step 3: VVPAT Verification (5 Random Stations)
- Supreme Court 2019 mandate: 5 random stations per constituency
- VVPAT compartment opened under videography; each slip counted
- If mismatch with EVM: VVPAT count prevails

### Step 4: Tabulation & Declaration
- Results entered into tabulation software; round-wise announcements made
- Form 20 declares final result; Form 21C/21E certificate of election issued
- Counted EVMs re-sealed and retained 45 days (election petition period)
