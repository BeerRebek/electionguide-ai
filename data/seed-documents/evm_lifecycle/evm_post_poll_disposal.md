# EVM Post-Poll Storage & Disposal

## Post-Counting Storage
- EVMs re-sealed after counting and stored in strong rooms at counting centers
- Double-lock system: Returning Officer + Treasury Officer
- 24/7 CCTV surveillance maintained
- Retained for minimum **45 days** (period for filing election petitions)
- If petition filed, retained until final court disposal

## Return to Warehouses
- After clearance period, EVMs transported back to district warehouses
- Chain of custody documented in EVM Tracking Software
- All seals removed, machines undergo maintenance check
- Batteries recharged or replaced if needed
- Machines prepared for next election cycle

## Condemnation & Disposal
- EVMs older than **15 years** or with irreparable defects condemned
- Three-member condemnation committee including technical expert from BEL/ECIL
- Committee inspects each machine and records reason for condemnation
- **Destruction**: Physical shredding at authorized e-waste facility
- Entire process under videographic documentation
- Destruction certificate issued and archived at ECI headquarters
