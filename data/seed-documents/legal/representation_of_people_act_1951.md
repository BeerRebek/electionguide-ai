# Representation of the People Act, 1951

## Overview

The Representation of the People Act, 1951 (RPA 1951) is the primary legislation governing the conduct of elections to the House of the People (Lok Sabha) and the Legislative Assemblies and Legislative Councils of States. Along with the Representation of the People Act, 1950, it forms the bedrock of Indian electoral law.

## Key Parts and Provisions

### Part I — Preliminary
- **Section 1**: Short title — "The Representation of the People Act, 1951"
- **Section 2**: Definitions — Defines key terms including "candidate," "corrupt practice," "electoral right," "election," "government company," and "returning officer"

### Part II — Qualifications and Disqualifications for Membership
- **Section 3**: Qualifications for membership of the House of the People — A person must be a citizen of India and not less than 25 years of age
- **Section 4**: Qualifications for membership of the Council of States — Not less than 30 years of age
- **Section 5**: Qualifications for membership of a State Legislative Assembly — Citizen of India, not less than 25 years, registered as voter in any constituency in the state
- **Section 8**: Disqualification on conviction for certain offences — Conviction for offences involving moral turpitude, promoting enmity between groups, or violations under Prevention of Corruption Act leads to disqualification for 6 years from date of conviction
- **Section 8A**: Disqualification on ground of corrupt practices
- **Section 9**: Disqualification for dismissal for corruption or disloyalty
- **Section 9A**: Disqualification for Government contracts
- **Section 10**: Disqualification for office under Government company
- **Section 10A**: Disqualification for failure to lodge account of election expenses

### Part III — Notification of General Elections
- **Section 14**: Notification for general election to House of the People
- **Section 15**: Notification for general election to State Assembly

### Part IVA — Registration of Political Parties
- **Section 29A**: Registration with the Election Commission — Any association or body of individual citizens of India calling itself a political party may apply for registration. The application must contain the party's name, address, members of executive committee, and a declaration that the party will bear true faith and allegiance to the Constitution of India.

### Part V — Conduct of Elections
- **Section 30**: Appointment of dates for nominations, scrutiny, and poll
- **Section 33**: Presentation of nomination papers — Must be submitted between 11 AM and 3 PM on the designated dates
- **Section 34**: Deposit — Rs. 25,000 for Lok Sabha candidates (Rs. 12,500 for SC/ST candidates); Rs. 10,000 for State Assembly (Rs. 5,000 for SC/ST)
- **Section 36**: Scrutiny of nomination papers by the Returning Officer
- **Section 38**: Withdrawal of candidature
- **Section 39**: Procedure in contested and uncontested elections
- **Section 49**: Manner of voting at elections — "At every election where a poll is taken votes shall be given by ballot in such manner as may be prescribed, and no votes shall be received by proxy"
- **Section 61A**: Voting machines — "Notwithstanding anything contained in this Act or the rules made thereunder, the giving and recording of votes by voting machines may be adopted in such constituency or constituencies as the Election Commission may, having regard to the circumstances of each case, specify"

### Part VII — Corrupt Practices and Electoral Offences
- **Section 77**: Account of election expenses — Every candidate must maintain a day-to-day account of all expenditure between nomination and result date
- **Section 78**: Lodging of account with the District Election Officer within 30 days of election result
- **Section 123**: Corrupt practices — Includes bribery, undue influence, appeal to religion/race/caste, use of national symbols, and booth capturing
- **Section 125**: Promoting enmity between classes in connection with election
- **Section 126**: Prohibition of public meetings during the period of 48 hours ending with the hour fixed for conclusion of poll
- **Section 127**: Disturbances at election meetings
- **Section 128**: Maintenance of secrecy of voting
- **Section 130**: Removal of ballot papers from polling station
- **Section 134A**: Penalty for Government servants for acting as election agents, etc.
- **Section 134B**: Prohibition of going armed to or near a polling station
- **Section 135**: Gratification to voters — Penalty of imprisonment up to one year or fine or both

### Part VIII — Election Disputes
- **Section 80**: Election petitions — Can be presented to the High Court by any candidate or elector
- **Section 80A**: High Court to try election petitions
- **Section 81**: Presentation of petitions — Must be filed within 45 days from the date of election of the returned candidate
- **Section 86**: Trial of election petitions — Procedure
- **Section 98**: Decision of the High Court — May dismiss the petition, or declare the election of the returned candidate void, or declare the election void and the petitioner or any other candidate as duly elected
- **Section 100**: Grounds for declaring election to be void — Corrupt practice by the candidate or with consent, non-compliance with constitutional provisions, improper acceptance or rejection of nominations, result materially affected by non-compliance with Act or Rules
- **Section 116A**: Appeals to Supreme Court — Appealable from every order of the High Court

## Important Amendments

- **1966 Amendment**: Introduction of Section 29A for registration of political parties
- **1989 Amendment**: Addition of provisions related to booth capturing
- **2002 Amendment**: Section 33A — Mandatory disclosure of criminal cases, assets, liabilities, and educational qualifications by candidates in affidavits (Form 26)
- **2003 Amendment**: Introduction of NOTA option provisions
- **2013 Amendment**: Section 8(4) declared unconstitutional by Supreme Court in Lily Thomas v. Union of India — Immediate disqualification of convicted legislators without 3-month window
