# Rules 49A to 49X — EVM Polling Procedures

## Rule 49A — Design of Voting Machines
Every voting machine shall have a control unit and a balloting unit. The control unit is operated by the Presiding Officer and the balloting unit is placed inside the voting compartment for use by the voter.

## Rule 49B — Preparation of Voting Machine
- Names of candidates displayed on the balloting unit in the same order as listed by the Returning Officer
- The serial number, name, and election symbol of each candidate displayed against a blue button
- Control unit tested and sealed before dispatch to polling station

## Rule 49C — Arrangement at Polling Station
The control unit shall be placed in front of the Presiding Officer. The balloting unit shall be placed inside the voting compartment in such a way that it is visible to the voter but the act of pressing a button is secret.

## Rule 49D — Marked Copy of Electoral Roll
The Presiding Officer shall maintain a marked copy of the electoral roll recording every person who casts a vote.

## Rule 49E — Demonstration
Before the poll begins, the Presiding Officer shall demonstrate the machine to all polling agents present, proving it records no votes initially and the machine functions correctly.

## Rule 49F — Identification of Voters
1. Voter must present valid photo ID (EPIC, Aadhaar, etc.)
2. Presiding Officer or polling officer verifies identity against the electoral roll
3. Indelible ink applied to left forefinger
4. Voter signs or puts thumb impression in Form 17A (Register of Voters)

## Rule 49G — Challenging of Identity
If a polling agent challenges a voter's identity, the Presiding Officer holds a summary inquiry. The challenged voter may still vote after making a declaration and depositing a fee of Rs. 2.

## Rule 49H — Recording of Votes
1. The voter enters the voting compartment
2. Presses the blue button next to the candidate of choice on the balloting unit
3. A beep sounds confirming the vote is recorded
4. If VVPAT is attached, a paper slip is generated showing the candidate's serial number, name, and symbol for 7 seconds before dropping into the sealed VVPAT box
5. The voter exits the polling station

## Rule 49I — Account of Votes Recorded
After the poll, the Presiding Officer:
1. Prepares account of votes in Part I of Form 17C
2. Records total votes polled, test votes, and any special cases
3. Gets signatures of polling agents present

## Rule 49J — Sealing After Poll
1. The result section of the control unit is sealed with pink paper seal
2. The control unit is placed in its carrying case and sealed
3. The seal numbers are recorded in the Presiding Officer's diary
4. Candidate/agents may affix their own seals on the carrying case

## Rule 49M — Closing of Poll
At the hour fixed for the close of poll, the Presiding Officer refuses to issue any ballot to voters who have not already entered the polling station. Voters already inside the polling station at the closing hour are allowed to vote.

## Rule 49MA — VVPAT Paper Trail
When VVPAT is used, after a voter presses the button, the printer generates a paper slip displaying:
- Serial number of the candidate
- Name of the candidate
- Symbol of the candidate

The slip is visible through a transparent window for 7 seconds, after which it automatically drops into a sealed compartment. If the paper trail machine malfunctions, the Presiding Officer may order polling to continue using the EVM without VVPAT, and reports this immediately.

## Rule 49N — Transmission to Returning Officer
The Presiding Officer transmits to the Returning Officer:
1. The voting machine (control unit in sealed condition)
2. The VVPAT drop box (sealed)
3. Account of votes recorded (Form 17C Part I)
4. Sealed packets of used, unused, and challenged ballot papers (if any)
5. Other election papers and material
