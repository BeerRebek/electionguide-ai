# Rule 56D — Paper Trail (VVPAT)

## Full Text
"Where the Election Commission has directed the use of printer for generating a paper trail of every vote recorded, the procedure for voting at any election in the constituency or constituencies so specified, and any other matter connected therewith or incidental thereto, shall be subject to such modifications as may be specified by the Election Commission."

## VVPAT Mechanism

### How VVPAT Works
1. Voter presses button on balloting unit
2. EVM control unit sends signal to attached VVPAT printer
3. Printer generates a paper slip (75mm × 105mm) showing:
   - Serial number of the candidate
   - Name of the candidate
   - Symbol allotted to the candidate
4. Slip is visible through a transparent window for **7 seconds**
5. Slip automatically cuts and drops into a sealed drop box
6. A beep confirms vote recording on the EVM

### VVPAT Components
- **Printing module**: Thermal printer attached to the control unit
- **Transparent viewing window**: Allows voter to verify the printed slip
- **Drop box**: Sealed compartment that collects printed slips
- **VSDU (Voter Slip Display Unit)**: The transparent section

## Mandatory Verification (Since 2019)

### 5 Random Polling Stations Rule
As per the Supreme Court order in *N. Chandrababu Naidu v. Union of India*, VVPAT paper slips must be physically counted and compared with EVM results in **5 randomly selected polling stations per Assembly constituency** (or Assembly segment in case of Lok Sabha elections).

### Selection Process
1. After all EVMs are opened at the counting center
2. Before actual counting of electronic votes begins
3. Five polling stations are selected by lottery in the presence of candidates/agents
4. VVPAT slips from these 5 stations are physically counted
5. If the VVPAT count matches the EVM count, counting proceeds
6. If there is a discrepancy, the VVPAT count prevails for that polling station

## Malfunction Protocol
- If VVPAT malfunctions during polling, the Presiding Officer replaces it with a spare unit
- If no spare is available, polling may continue without VVPAT but this must be reported immediately
- Maximum 5 replacement attempts allowed per polling station
