# Conduct of Elections Rules, 1961

## Overview

The Conduct of Elections Rules, 1961 are subordinate legislation made under Sections 169 and 128 of the Representation of the People Act, 1951. They prescribe the detailed procedures and forms for the conduct of elections to Parliament and State Legislatures.

## Part I — Preliminary (Rules 1-3)
- **Rule 1**: Short title and commencement
- **Rule 2**: Definitions
- **Rule 3**: Forms — All forms appended to these rules shall be used for the purposes indicated

## Part II — Election Machinery (Rules 4-13)

### Returning Officers and Staff
- **Rule 4**: Appointment of Returning Officers (ROs) — The Election Commission appoints or designates a person as the RO for each constituency
- **Rule 5**: Appointment of Assistant Returning Officers (AROs)
- **Rule 6**: Appointment of presiding officers and polling officers for each polling station
- **Rule 9**: Polling agents — Each candidate may appoint one polling agent and one relief agent for each polling station
- **Rule 10**: Counting agents — Each candidate may appoint counting agents not exceeding the number of tables fixed for counting

## Part IV — Poll (Rules 26-57)

### Polling Station Setup
- **Rule 26**: Design of ballot papers and arrangement of names
- **Rule 29**: Ballot boxes — Specifications and sealing procedures
- **Rule 30**: Arrangement of polling stations — Must have a voting compartment for secrecy

### Voting Procedures (Pre-EVM)
- **Rule 35**: Issue of ballot papers to voters
- **Rule 38**: Recording of votes — Voter marks ballot paper with instrument supplied, folds it, and inserts into ballot box

### EVM Voting Procedures (Rules 49A-49X)
- **Rule 49A**: Design of voting machines — The balloting unit and control unit
- **Rule 49B**: Preparation of voting machine — Names of candidates displayed on balloting unit in same order as listed by RO
- **Rule 49C**: Arrangement at polling station where voting machine is used
- **Rule 49E**: Demonstration of voting machine — Before poll begins, presiding officer demonstrates the machine to polling agents present
- **Rule 49H**: Recording of votes — Voter presses the blue button on balloting unit against the candidate of choice
- **Rule 49I**: Account of votes recorded — After poll, presiding officer seals the control unit, prepares account in Form 17C
- **Rule 49J**: Sealing of voting machines after poll — Control unit sealed with special seal in presence of candidates/agents
- **Rule 49K**: Procedure on adjournment of poll
- **Rule 49L**: Procedure on fresh poll (countermanding)
- **Rule 49M**: Closing of poll — No voter admitted after official closing hour if already in queue at that time
- **Rule 49N**: Account of votes recorded and transmission to RO

### VVPAT Provisions
- **Rule 49MA**: Voter verifiable paper audit trail (VVPAT) — Printer attached to control unit generates a paper slip for each vote cast displaying serial number, name, and symbol of candidate voted for
- **Rule 56D**: Paper trail — "Where the Election Commission has directed the use of printer for generating a paper trail of every vote recorded, the procedure for voting shall be subject to such modifications as may be specified by the Election Commission"

## Part V — Counting of Votes (Rules 54-66)

- **Rule 54**: Time and place for counting — The RO fixes date, time, and place
- **Rule 55**: Counting of votes where ballot papers are used
- **Rule 56**: Counting of votes where voting machines are used
- **Rule 56A**: Scrutiny and inspection of paper trail
- **Rule 56C**: Verification of paper trail — Mandatory VVPAT verification in 5 randomly selected polling stations per constituency
- **Rule 57**: Equality of votes — If after counting, there is equality of votes between candidates, the RO decides by drawing of lots
- **Rule 63**: Declaration of results — RO declares the candidate with highest votes as elected
- **Rule 64**: Report of result — Form 20 sent to Election Commission and the appropriate authority
- **Rule 66**: Grant of certificate of election — Form 22

## Part VI — Registration of Electors (Rules 15-25)
- **Rule 26B**: Forms for registration — Form 6, Form 6A (overseas), Form 7, Form 8

## Part IX — Safety and Security
- **Rule 92**: Storage of ballot papers and voting machines — Must be stored in strong rooms under proper security
- **Rule 93**: Production and inspection of election papers — Papers kept for one year and then destroyed unless required for an election petition
- **Rule 94**: Disposal after period — After the period under Rule 93, papers may be destroyed under orders of the Election Commission
