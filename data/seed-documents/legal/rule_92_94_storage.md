# Rules 92 & 94 — Storage and Post-Poll Procedures

## Rule 92 — Custody of Election Papers
All papers and materials relating to an election shall remain in the custody of the officer designated by the Election Commission (typically the District Election Officer) and shall be kept in such place and under such conditions as may be directed by the Election Commission.

### Materials under custody:
1. Electronic Voting Machines (sealed control units)
2. VVPAT drop boxes (sealed)
3. Marked copies of electoral rolls
4. Counterfoils of ballot papers
5. Register of voters (Form 17A)
6. Account of votes (Form 17C)
7. Declaration by voters
8. Tendered ballot papers
9. Any challenged ballot papers

## Rule 93 — Production and Inspection
- No person shall open or inspect any sealed election material except under the order of a competent court or tribunal
- Election papers shall be preserved for a minimum period of **one year** from the date of declaration of result
- If an election petition is pending, the papers shall be preserved until the petition is finally disposed of

## Rule 94 — Disposal After Prescribed Period
- After the one-year period (or after election petition disposal), the Superintendent/District Election Officer may cause the election papers to be destroyed
- Destruction must be carried out under the orders of the Election Commission
- Proper records of destruction must be maintained

## Strong Room Procedures

### Three-Tier Security System
1. **Inner room**: EVMs and VVPAT boxes stored on shelves, sorted by constituency/polling station
2. **Access control**: Double lock — one key with District Magistrate, one with Returning Officer
3. **Armed guard**: 24/7 CRPF/CISF or state armed police presence

### Sealing Protocol
- Strong room door sealed with paper seals affixed by candidate representatives
- CCTV surveillance mandatory (post-2019)
- Visitor log maintained
- Any entry requires written authorization from ECI with prior notice to candidates/parties
