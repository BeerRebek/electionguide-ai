# Pink Paper Seals

## What Are Pink Paper Seals?
- Tamper-evident paper seals with unique serial numbers
- Made of special security paper that tears if removal is attempted
- Printed by India Security Press, Nashik (same facility that prints currency)
- Distributed to DEO offices under secure chain of custody

## When They Are Applied
1. **After First Level Checking**: Seals applied in presence of party representatives
2. **After Commissioning**: Additional seals after candidate loading
3. **After Mock Poll**: Final seals before actual voting begins

## Signature Protocol
- Each pink paper seal is signed by:
  - BEL/ECIL engineer (after FLC)
  - Representatives of all contesting candidates
  - Presiding Officer
- Agents record seal numbers for their polling stations
- Seal numbers cross-referenced in Seal Register

## Verification on Poll Day
- Presiding Officer checks all seals before mock poll
- Candidates' agents independently verify seal integrity
- If any seal is broken, tampered, or missing:
  - Machine is immediately set aside
  - Reserve machine deployed
  - Incident reported to Returning Officer
  - Written record in Presiding Officer's diary

## Importance in Chain of Custody
- Seals provide physical evidence that no unauthorized access occurred
- Multiple signatures ensure no single party can tamper undetected
- Serial number tracking creates complete audit trail
- Forms part of the multi-layered EVM security architecture
