# Strong Room Procedures
- After poll, EVMs transported under armed escort to strong room
- Double lock: District Magistrate holds one key, Returning Officer holds other
- Paper seals affixed by candidate representatives on the door
- 24/7 armed guard (Central Armed Police Force)
- CCTV with continuous recording
- Visitor log maintained
- Any entry requires prior written authorization from ECI with notice to candidates

# Videography Protocols
- Mandatory videography at all stages: FLC, commissioning, polling day, counting
- Webcasting of polling in sensitive booths
- All counting centers videographed
- Video evidence preserved for election petitions
- Mobile phones banned in counting halls for candidates/agents during counting
