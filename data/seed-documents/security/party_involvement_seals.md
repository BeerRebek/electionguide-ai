# Political Party Involvement in Election Process
- Party representatives present at First Level Checking
- Randomization of EVMs observed by party agents
- Commissioning signed by party representatives
- Mock poll witnessed by polling agents
- Polling agents present throughout polling day
- Pink paper seals signed by agents on EVM result section after polling
- Strong room sealing witnessed by agents
- Counting agents present at every counting table

# Pink Paper Seals
- Special green/pink paper seals applied to the result section of the Control Unit after polling
- Signed by the Presiding Officer and all willing polling agents
- Prevents unauthorized access to the result display
- Seal integrity verified before counting begins
- Any broken or tampered seal triggers an inquiry
