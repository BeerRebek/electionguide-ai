# Campaigning & Silence Period

## Campaign Period Rules

### Permitted Activities
- Public meetings and rallies (with police permission)
- Door-to-door canvassing
- Distribution of printed pamphlets and posters
- Social media campaigning
- Authorized advertisements in newspapers and TV
- Use of vehicles for campaigning (with permit)

### Model Code of Conduct Restrictions
- No appeal to caste or communal feelings
- No use of government machinery for campaigning
- No distribution of liquor or cash
- No defacement of public property
- No campaign spending beyond prescribed limits
- All advertisements must carry "paid for by" disclosure

### Expense Limits (2024)
- **Lok Sabha**: ₹95 lakh (varies by state)
- **State Assembly**: ₹40 lakh (varies by state)
- All expenses must be recorded in daily expenditure register
- ECI's expenditure monitoring teams verify compliance

## Silence Period (Section 126)

### What Is Prohibited (48 hours before poll)
1. **Public meetings** of any kind
2. **Processions and rallies**
3. **Loudspeaker use** for election purposes
4. **Election broadcasts** on TV, radio, and cable networks
5. **Display of election matter** by means of cinematograph, television, or similar apparatus
6. **Social media**: Guidelines restrict paid political advertisements

### What Is Still Allowed
- Personal conversations and private discussions
- Static display of posters/banners (already placed before silence period)
- News coverage (editorial, not paid content)
- Candidate's personal social media posts (non-promotional)

### Penalties for Violation
- Section 126: Imprisonment up to **2 years** and/or fine
- Filing of FIR by law enforcement
- ECI can issue show-cause notice and censure
- Repeat offenders may face deregistration of party symbol
