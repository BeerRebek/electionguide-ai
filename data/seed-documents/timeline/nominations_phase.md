# Nominations Phase

## Filing Nominations

### Requirements
- **Nomination form** (Form 2A/2B) signed by candidate and proposer(s)
- **Security deposit**: ₹25,000 for Lok Sabha; ₹10,000 for State Assembly (half for SC/ST candidates)
- **Form 26 Affidavit**: Mandatory disclosure of:
  - Criminal cases (pending and convicted)
  - Assets and liabilities (self, spouse, dependents)
  - Educational qualifications
  - PAN and Income Tax returns for last 5 years
- **Proposer signatures**: 10 registered electors from the constituency
- **Party letter** (for party candidates): Official authorization from party

### Timeline
- Nominations open on the date of election notification
- Last date for filing: 7th day after notification (or as specified by ECI)
- Multiple nominations allowed (up to 4 per candidate)

## Scrutiny of Nominations
- Conducted on the day after the last nomination date
- **Returning Officer** examines each nomination for:
  - Completeness of form
  - Validity of proposers (registered in the constituency?)
  - Security deposit receipt
  - Candidate eligibility (age, citizenship, disqualifications)
- Candidates can raise objections to other nominations
- RO's decision is final at this stage (appealable to court later)

## Withdrawal of Candidature
- **Withdrawal period**: 2 days after scrutiny
- Candidate must personally submit withdrawal notice to Returning Officer
- No withdrawal allowed after the deadline
- List of contesting candidates published after withdrawal deadline
- Candidate symbols allotted (reserved symbols for recognized parties, free symbols for independents/unrecognized)
