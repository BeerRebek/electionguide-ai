# Electoral Roll Preparation Phase

## Continuous and Summary Revision

### Continuous Revision
- Applications accepted year-round via Form 6, 6A, 7, 8
- Monthly integration of accepted applications into the roll
- Online submission available at voters.eci.gov.in and Voter Helpline App

### Special Summary Revision (Before Elections)
1. **Draft roll publication**: Based on January 1 as qualifying date
2. **House-to-house survey**: Booth Level Officers (BLOs) verify every household
3. **Claims and objections period**: 30 days for public to file additions/corrections/deletions
4. **Disposal of claims**: Electoral Registration Officer decides on each application
5. **Final roll publication**: After all claims resolved

## Key Forms
- **Form 6**: New voter registration (18+ as of January 1)
- **Form 6A**: Overseas (NRI) voter registration
- **Form 7**: Objection to inclusion (request for deletion)
- **Form 8**: Correction of entries (name, address, photo, etc.)

## Qualifying Criteria
- Indian citizen, 18 years of age as of January 1 of the revision year
- Ordinary resident of the constituency
- Not disqualified under Section 16 of RP Act (unsound mind, corrupt practices conviction, etc.)
- NRI citizens can register under Form 6A with passport as proof
