# Polling & Counting Phase

## Poll Day Operations
- Polling hours: 7:00 AM to 6:00 PM (extended in some regions)
- Voters in queue at closing time allowed to vote
- Mandatory mock poll before actual voting begins
- Voter identification via EPIC or 12 approved alternative IDs
- Indelible ink applied before voting

## Post-Poll Transport
- EVMs sealed in carrying cases with tamper-evident seals
- Transported under armed escort to counting center
- GPS-tracked vehicles; route pre-approved by DEO
- Strong room sealed with double locks on arrival
- 24/7 CCTV surveillance of strong room

## Counting Day
- Typically 3-5 days after poll day (set by ECI schedule)
- Strong room opened in presence of candidates and observers
- Table-by-table counting; each table handles one EVM at a time
- VVPAT verification: 5 random stations per constituency
- Round-wise results announced; final result declared via Form 20
- Certificate of election (Form 21C/21E) issued to winner

## Key Forms
- **Form 17A**: Register of voters (maintained during polling)
- **Form 17C Part I**: Account of votes at polling station
- **Form 17C Part II**: Counting-day result per station
- **Form 20**: Declaration of result
- **Form 21C/21E**: Certificate of election
